Privacy policy

What we collect

Name, mobile number, class, email address, test answers and results, attendance, and payment details such as amount, date and Razorpay payment reference.

Why

To create your account, run tests, show your results, issue receipts and contact you about your studies and payments.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers needed to run the website (such as Google Firebase, Razorpay and our email service) or when the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to correct or delete your data by writing to stemcoaching.in@gmail.com.
